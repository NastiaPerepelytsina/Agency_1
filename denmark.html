    • Denmark

    Copenhagen is worth visiting for its beautiful canals, ancient architecture, and the famous Little Mermaid statue, where you can experience the atmosphere of Danish culture. In Aarhus, tourists can see the fascinating open-air museum Den Gamle By and modern architecture. The city of Odense is known as the birthplace of the famous writer Hans Christian Andersen, where you can visit a museum dedicated to his fairy tales. In Aalborg, travelers can stroll along the waterfront, see historic buildings, and visit Aalborg Zoo. Each of these cities showcases a different side of Denmark—from royal history and literature to contemporary culture and a cozy Scandinavian atmosphere. 🇩🇰✨
