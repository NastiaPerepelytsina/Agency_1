Where can you go?

Denmark

Denmark is a cozy Scandinavian country that combines rich history, modern design, and unforgettable nature. Even a simple tourist will find something exciting here - from fairytale castles and Viking heritage to lively cities and peaceful seaside landscapes. Copenhagen is the perfect starting point, but the whole country is full of treasures worth visiting.

  • Copenhagen
  • Aarhus
  • Odense
  • Aalborg

Australia

Australia is a land of contrasts – from bustling modern cities to vast wild landscapes. A tourist can enjoy the vibrant lifestyle of Sydney and relax on golden beaches. Wherever you go, Australia is a mix of adventure, relaxation, and unforgettable experiences.

  • Sydney
  • Uluru
  • Melbourne
  • Gold Coast

Finland

Finland is a magical destination where untouched nature meets modern Nordic charm. Visitors can experience thousands of sparkling lakes, mysterious forests, and the breathtaking Northern Lights. In winter, it’s a wonderland of husky safaris, reindeer rides, and cozy stays in glass igloos. Finland offers peace, adventure, and unforgettable moments in every season.

  • Rovaniemi & Santa Claus Village (Lapland)
  • Helsinki
  • Porvoo
  • Lake Saimaa

Poland

Poland is a country where medieval charm meets modern energy. Travelers can wander through colorful old towns, discover royal castles, and enjoy hearty traditional cuisine. From vibrant cities to breathtaking natural landscapes, Poland offers something for every kind of explorer. It’s a destination full of history, culture, and unforgettable memories waiting to be made.

  • Wawel Castle
  • Wieliczka Salt Mine
  • Zakopane & The Tatra Mountains
  • Old Town of Krakow

Germany

Germany is a country where medieval castles meet modern cities, offering travelers a rich blend of history and culture. Visitors can stroll through romantic old towns, taste world-famous beer, and enjoy vibrant festivals like Oktoberfest. From fairytale landscapes to futuristic architecture, every region has its own charm. Whether you seek art, history, or nature, Germany has something unforgettable for you.

  • Oktoberfest (Munich)
  • Schloss Neuschwanstein
  • Black Forest (Schwarzwald)
  • Cologne Cathedral (Kölner Dom)
